[3] file=public/anim/figs/heartcut.png (landscape 4:3, 스타일 B)
A human heart cut open in a front-facing coronal section so all four internal chambers are visible at once as hollow spaces. The two upper chambers are smaller with visibly thin muscular walls; the two lower chambers are larger with visibly thick muscular walls, and the wall of the lower left chamber is clearly the thickest of all. Between each upper and lower chamber sits a thin flap valve, and another thin flap valve sits where each lower chamber opens into its outgoing great vessel at the top. Large vessels enter and leave at the top of the heart. Deep red-pink muscle, chamber interiors darker, valve flaps pale cream. Whole heart shown, no arrows, no flow marks.

Most numerous by far: many bright red flattened discs with a dimpled sunken center, like tiny doughnuts without a hole.
