# Hand-drawn stick figure comic panel, black ink lines on warm off-white paper,
# exactly one accent color: teal, used sparingly where the prompt says.
# Absolutely NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO Chinese characters, NO speech bubbles,
# NO captions anywhere. Characters ACT like they are talking: open mouth (small oval),
# expressive hand gestures. Comedic acting is allowed and encouraged where the prompt marks GAG:
# exaggerated poses, big reactions, falling over backwards, sweat drops, bulging wide eyes,
# jaw dropping — but all historical objects, clothing and facts must stay accurate; never make
# the scene absurd beyond what the prompt describes, and NO anachronistic props inside
# historical scenes.
# CONQUEST RULE (absolute): never draw battle, blood, weapons striking people, or death.
# Conquest and disaster are conveyed only through gates, processions, architecture and nature.
# RELIGION RULE (absolute): never depict founders or objects of worship of any religion —
# no deities, no worship statues, no crosses or other worship symbols. Religious buildings
# appear as plain building exteriors only.
# Keep the upper third of the panel mostly empty white space above the characters' heads
# (the app overlays Korean speech bubbles there). Main subjects centered horizontally.
# Simple, charming, educational middle-school history comic. 4:3 landscape panel.
