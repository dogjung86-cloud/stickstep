스타일 블록 A (모든 프롬프트 앞에 붙일 공통 스타일):
Photorealistic photograph, natural lighting, shallow depth of field, clean composition, single clear subject.
ABSOLUTELY NO text, NO numbers, NO letters, NO logos, NO watermarks, NO scale bars anywhere in the image.
No people, no hands, no faces, no human body parts (including reflections). Square 1:1 aspect ratio. High detail, realistic textures.
Any instrument display panel must be completely blank (no digits, no tick marks, no readings).
